### Added
- Starbase Sheets: sheets have a size, as in Google Sheets. New sheets have 1000 rows and 26
  columns; deleting the last rows or columns makes the sheet smaller, and **Add [1000] more rows at
  the bottom** under the last row makes it bigger again. The size is kept in the .xlsx file (Excel
  ignores it and shows its full grid).

### Changed
- Starbase Sheets: a new sheet (the + button, Insert › Sheet, Shift+F11) goes right after the
  sheet you are on, as in Google Sheets, for everyone editing the file; someone on a later sheet
  stays on their sheet.
