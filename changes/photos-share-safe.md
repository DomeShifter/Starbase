### Added
- Photos shared by link, or downloaded by someone other than the owner, are sent without where they were taken (the owner can untick "Remove location when sharing" per share). The owner's own download is the original.
- Photo viewer: an Info panel (the i button, or I) with date taken, camera, size and, for people allowed to see it, location; an "Open with…" button.

### Changed
- Photo viewer: Escape now closes the Info panel first (like Google Photos); a second Escape closes the viewer.
