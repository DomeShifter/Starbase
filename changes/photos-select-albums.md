### Added
- Photos: select pictures the Google Photos way: a round tick on hover, Ctrl-click, Shift-click for a range, Ctrl-A, Esc to clear; a bar on top while pictures are selected (Add to album, Favourite, Download, Move to bin, Remove from album inside an album, Select all).
- Photos: right-click a picture for Open, Add to album…, Favourite, Download, Info, Move to bin (and Remove from album inside an album); it acts on the whole selection when the picture is in it.
- Photos: "Add to album…" picker with search, Recent albums and New album (type a name, Enter); a note says how many were added and offers Open album. Pictures already in the album are not added twice.
- Photos: "Add photos" inside an album: choose from the Photos folder; pictures already there show ticked.

### Changed
- Photos: the old "Select" / "Done selecting" pills and the row of album pills are gone (replaced by the above).

### Fixed
- Photos: Favourite now knows when a picture in a folder inside (or in an album) is already a favourite, so it takes the star off instead of adding it again. Photos listings now say which pictures you starred.
